# Customer Discovery Kit: Clinical Trial Planning Simulation

Goal: find out in about 10 conversations whether trial-planning simulation is a top-three pain point
for someone with budget, and what they use today. Do not pitch. Listen.

## Who to talk to (target 10)

| Role | Org type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Head / VP of Clinical Operations | Small-mid biotech (Phase II-III) | Owns enrollment timelines and budget |
| Feasibility lead / Site selection manager | CRO | Builds enrollment plans for many sponsors |
| Clinical supply / IRT lead | Biotech or CRO | Overage and stockout pain |
| Biostatistician | Biotech or CRO | Power sensitivity to dropout and enrollment |
| Clinical trial manager | Biotech | Lives with the timeline slipping |

Aim for at least 4 biotechs, 3 CROs, and 3 mixed roles. Avoid top-20 pharma at first: they build in-house
and their buying cycles are slow.

Sources for names: LinkedIn search, conference speaker lists (SCOPE, DIA), ClinicalTrials.gov sponsors of
active Phase II/III studies, warm intros.

## Outreach message (short)

> Hi [Name], I'm researching how clinical operations teams plan enrollment timelines and site portfolios
> before protocol lock. Not selling anything. Could I ask you 20 minutes of questions about how you do it
> today? Happy to share what I learn across interviews.

## Interview script (20-30 min)

Open
1. What's your role, and what trials are you working on or planning now?

Current process (past behavior, not opinions)
2. Walk me through the last time you built an enrollment or timeline plan. What did you use?
3. Where did the numbers (recruitment rate, site activation time, screen failure, dropout) come from?
4. How far off was the plan from what happened? What caused it?
5. Who else is involved: biostatistics, supply, finance? Where do handoffs break down?

Pain and cost
6. What's the most painful or expensive planning mistake you've seen? What did it cost?
7. How long does it take to evaluate one alternative scenario (more sites, a looser criterion)?
8. What have you tried to fix this? Why didn't it stick?

Tools and budget
9. Which tools do you use today (spreadsheets, Citeline, Medidata, Certara, in-house)? What do you like or dislike?
10. Who would own a purchase decision like this, and which budget does it come from?
11. What would make you trust a simulation output enough to change a plan?

Close
12. If a tool did this well, what would it need to do in the first month to be worth paying for?
13. Who else should I talk to?

## Scoring (fill in after each call)

| Interviewee | Role / org | Pain rank (1-5) | Current tool | Has budget? | Would pilot? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Kill / continue criteria (set before calls)

- **Continue** if at least 3 of 10 say planning is a top-three pain point and at least 2 would pilot with their own data.
- **Pivot** if the pain is real but sits in a different step (for example site selection or protocol design only).
- **Stop** if fewer than 3 of 10 care or nobody can name a budget owner.

## Red flags to listen for

- "We already do this in-house in Excel and it's fine."
- "We'd never trust a third-party model for this."
- Pain exists but no one owns the budget.
